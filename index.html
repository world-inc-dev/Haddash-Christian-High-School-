About Dashan

A School Designed For Growth

Dashan International School is located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division, Uganda. The school brings together international and national learning pathways in one school community.

Learners can pursue the Cambridge International Curriculum or the Uganda National Curriculum, supported by academic development, practical learning and a broad range of opportunities.

  • ✓ Cambridge International Curriculum
  • ✓ Uganda National Curriculum
  • ✓ Day & Boarding
  • ✓ Arts & Science
  • ✓ English, French & Chinese
Learning Pathways

Two Curriculum Pathways

Dashan International School provides both Cambridge International and Uganda National Curriculum pathways.

Cambridge International Curriculum

An international pathway supporting learners from foundational education through advanced international qualifications.

  • Cambridge Primary
  • Cambridge Lower Secondary
  • Cambridge IGCSE
  • Cambridge International AS & A Level

Uganda National Curriculum

The national pathway covering O’ Level and A’ Level education with Arts and Science options.

  • O’ Level — Senior 1 to Senior 4
  • A’ Level — Senior 5 to Senior 6
  • Arts
  • Science
