About Our School

Education with purpose, opportunity and growth.

Dashan International School is a day and boarding school located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division, Uganda.

The school provides learners with a choice of Cambridge International Curriculum and Uganda National Curriculum pathways, supporting both international and national academic progression.

  • Cambridge International Curriculum
  • Uganda National Curriculum
  • Arts & Science pathways
  • Day & Boarding
  • English, French & Chinese
Academic Pathways

Two Curriculum Pathways. One School.

Learners can follow either the Cambridge International pathway or the Uganda National Curriculum pathway.

Cambridge International Curriculum

An internationally recognised pathway designed to support strong academic progression and global opportunities.

  • Cambridge Primary
  • Cambridge Lower Secondary
  • Cambridge IGCSE
  • Cambridge International AS & A Level

Uganda National Curriculum

A national pathway supporting learners through O’ Level and A’ Level education.

  • O’ Level — Senior 1 to Senior 4
  • A’ Level — Senior 5 to Senior 6
  • Arts
  • Science
