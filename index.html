About Us

A School Designed for Possibility

Education that combines academic preparation, character development and practical learning.

Welcome to Dashan International School

Dashan International School is a learning institution located in Opuyo, Soroti, serving learners through a combination of international and national educational pathways.

Our school provides access to the Cambridge International Curriculum alongside the Uganda National Curriculum, giving families educational options suited to different academic goals.

  • Cambridge International Curriculum
  • Uganda National Curriculum
  • O & A Level Arts and Science
  • Day and Boarding
  • French included as part of learning
  • Supportive academic environment

Academic Pathways

Two Curriculum Options. One School Community.

Dashan International School brings together international and national curriculum pathways under one school.

PATHWAY 01

Cambridge International Curriculum

An internationally recognised curriculum designed to develop independent thinking, knowledge and academic skills.

  • International academic pathway
  • Strong emphasis on understanding
  • Broad subject opportunities
  • Preparation for further education
PATHWAY 02

Uganda National Curriculum

A national curriculum pathway supporting learners through Uganda's education system and examination structure.

  • Uganda National Curriculum
  • O & A Level Arts and Science
  • Academic and practical learning
  • Preparation for national examinations
