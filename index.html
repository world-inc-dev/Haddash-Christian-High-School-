Learn. Grow. Thrive.

Dashan International School Uganda is located in Opuyo, Soroti East Division and provides a modern educational environment for learners.

The school combines the Cambridge International Curriculum with the Uganda National Curriculum, giving learners access to a broad academic foundation.

  • Location: Opuyo – Soroti East Division
  • Curriculum: Cambridge + Uganda National Curriculum
  • School Type: Day & Boarding
  • Level: O' & A' Level — Arts & Science
  • MOES Registration: PSS/H/153
