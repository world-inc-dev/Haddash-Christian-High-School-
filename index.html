About Dashan International School

A place to learn, grow and prepare for the future.

Dashan International School is located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division.

The school offers two curriculum pathways: the Uganda National Curriculum and the Cambridge International Curriculum.

Students can pursue Arts and Science pathways with educational opportunities designed to support their academic development and future goals.

The school also provides Day and Boarding options and includes French among its educational offerings.
