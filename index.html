Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from parents.

What curriculum does Haddassah offer?

The provided school information states that Haddassah offers Ugandan and Cambridge curriculum, with O’ and A’ Level Arts and Science subjects.

Does Haddassah offer boarding?

Yes. The official information provided states that Haddassah offers both Day and Boarding.

Is French available at Haddassah?

Yes. French is listed as inclusive in the school's provided information.

What curriculum does Dashan offer?

Dashan International School is presented on this website as the Cambridge curriculum pathway.

Where is Haddassah located?

Haddassah Christian High School is located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division.
