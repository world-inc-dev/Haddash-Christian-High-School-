About Dashan

Education for a Changing World

Where Excellence Meets Opportunity

Dashan International School is a day and boarding school located in Opuyo, Soroti East Division, Uganda.

The school provides learners with the opportunity to follow either the Cambridge International Curriculum or the Uganda National Curriculum while developing academic, practical and personal skills.

Our learning environment supports students in Arts and Science subjects at O' and A' Level, together with French and digital learning opportunities.

Our Curriculum

Two Educational Pathways

Learners can pursue an educational pathway that matches their academic goals while studying in one school community.

Cambridge International Curriculum

An internationally recognised pathway designed to develop independent thinking, academic confidence and preparation for further education.

  • International academic standards
  • Arts and Science pathways
  • Independent learning
  • Critical thinking
  • Preparation for further education

Uganda National Curriculum

A Uganda-based curriculum pathway supporting learners through secondary education while developing strong academic and practical foundations.

  • Uganda National Curriculum
  • O' & A' Level
  • Arts and Science
  • Academic development
  • Practical learning
