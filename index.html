About Our School

Education With Purpose

Dashan International School is committed to providing an environment where learners can develop academically, socially and personally.

Located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division, the school offers both Cambridge International Curriculum and Uganda National Curriculum pathways.

Our approach combines academic learning with discipline, character development, practical skills and preparation for future opportunities.

Our Curriculum

Two Curriculum Pathways

Families can choose an educational pathway that suits the learner's academic goals and future plans.

🌍

Cambridge International Curriculum

An internationally recognised curriculum designed to develop strong academic foundations, independent thinking, problem-solving and preparation for further education.

🇺🇬

Uganda National Curriculum

A Uganda-based curriculum pathway supporting learners through the national education system while developing academic knowledge, practical abilities and character.

Academic Programme

Learning For Every Stage

Our academic programme supports learners across O' and A' Level, with both Arts and Science options.

O' Level

Strong foundations in academic subjects, skills development and preparation for advanced study.

A' Level

Advanced study designed to prepare learners for higher education and future professional pathways.

Arts & Science

Learners can pursue academic interests across Arts and Science subject pathways.
