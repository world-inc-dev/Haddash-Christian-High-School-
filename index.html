ABOUT DASHAN

Education With Purpose

Dashan International School is a learning community in Opuyo – Soroti East Division committed to academic excellence, character development and preparing learners for a changing world.

Our school brings together the Cambridge International Curriculum and Uganda National Curriculum, providing families with different academic pathways within one school community.

Location: Opuyo – Soroti East Division, Uganda
MOES Registration: PSS/H/153
School Arrangement: Day & Boarding
Motto: Grace to Grow, Space to Thrive
