ABOUT US

Education With Purpose

Dashan International School is a school community in Opuyo – Soroti East Division committed to creating an environment where learners can develop academically, socially and personally.

The school combines the Cambridge International Curriculum with the Uganda National Curriculum, giving families flexibility while maintaining a strong focus on academic growth and character development.

Location: Opuyo – Soroti East Division, Uganda
Registration: MOES Reg. No. PSS/H/153
School Arrangement: Day & Boarding
