About Dashan

Education for growth, confidence and opportunity.

Dashan International School Uganda is a school in Opuyo, Soroti East Division, providing learners with access to both international and Uganda national educational pathways.

Our learning environment supports academic development, character formation, practical skills and preparation for future opportunities.

  • Cambridge International Curriculum
  • Uganda National Curriculum
  • Day and Boarding options
  • Arts and Science pathways
  • English, French and Chinese language opportunities
