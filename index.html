About Our School

Dashan International School

Dashan International School is located in Opuyo – Soroti East Division.

The school offers both the Uganda National Curriculum and Cambridge International Curriculum, giving families a choice of educational pathway.

The school offers O' and A' Level Arts and Science subjects and provides both Day and Boarding options.

French is also included as part of the school's educational offering.
